# 06 Mistakes already made

Read once before planning a film. Each line is something that was built, shown and rejected.

## Purpose

- **A film as the whole review.** Watched once, with one or two questions and no schedule, it gave a mood and no memory. Most topics of the month were not covered at all. The pack and the cards come first.
- **Spending on what does not teach.** Hours of rendering went into a room around the content. Ask of every layer what the viewer understands because of it; if the answer is nothing, do not build it.

## Content

- **One note standing in for a topic.** The topic had a dozen notes over six months; the film used the one touched that month and came out shallow. Read the group.
- **Stopping at names.** The notes reached method and cases; the film showed titles and one-liners.
- **Edit history as content.** Dates, authors and counts of changes read like an audit.
- **Choosing between two notes that disagree.** Report it; do not settle it for the author.
- **A clue that is the answer.**
- **Numbers that differ between two screens.** Recompute from the notes, and keep them the same everywhere.
- **Writing an intention as if it had happened**, or giving an outside example without marking it.
- **Other people's results standing in for the author's.** A plan not yet carried out was shown with quoted case numbers as if it had reached real use. Say whose numbers they are and that the plan is untried.
- **A note listed under two topics counted as links between them.** Eight "links" all came from one shared note. Shared notes are reported apart.

## Order

- **Opening on the full relation map.** Too much at once; nothing on it meant anything yet. Open with a few points; show the map last.
- **A new layout for every segment.** Each screen was fine and the whole was a pile. One skeleton, kept visible, with fixed places and colours.
- **Lines without a relation**, and relation labels laid over nodes until neither could be read. One relation at a time, in a place kept for it.
- **Every topic listed on one screen to be read.** Show the few that matter now; the rest are texture.
- **A short film that hid the questions it skipped.** The viewer lost the map. Show the whole skeleton and dim what this film does not walk.
- **A skeleton colour reused for something else** (a category, "written this month", an underline). The colour stopped meaning its question.

## Picture

- **Content inside a pictured screen** (a laptop on a desk, a monitor in a room, cards on a wall): a screen inside a screen. The content got smaller and the surroundings said nothing.
- **Text placed in a scene and called a video.** The picture must show the mechanism.
- **Signposts.** A still that only says which subject this is. Hide the text and look.
- **Type too small after scaling**, text broken in the middle of a word, a question mark alone on a line. Check stills at full size.
- **A dim screen during recall** that showed less than before. Dim what is irrelevant, keep the question and its parts lit.

## Process

- **Rendering before looking.** The full film was rendered, then the type turned out too small. Stills first.
- **Trusting a pattern's layout with long text.** A wrapped line ran into the next label; two relations on one question sat on top of each other. The stills are the check, not the pattern.
- **The same id in two svgs.** Arrowheads resolved to the first, hidden one and never appeared, in every film, until someone looked for them. A thing that should be on screen and is not is a bug, not a style.
- **Timing changed, cue sheet not regenerated.** The score no longer matched.
- **A bed with no melody under a ten-minute film.** It measured as present and was heard as "no background sound at all". Then samples with a melody at -19 LUFS were "too loud" for reading. The score needs a melody and a low level, both.
- **Deepening git history with file contents.** Gigabytes of old videos. Fetch history without blobs.
- **Two encodes at slow settings.** The intermediate only needs to be fast.
- **Asking whether an idea is good instead of showing it.** Make a short sample and let the person look.
